How these numbers are made

Rates come from the Bank of Tanzania (provider BOTA) through the free Frankfurter API and are saved by the project's daily Python run into a SQLite table. The bank publishes no rate on weekends and holidays, so those days repeat the previous rate until a real one arrives. The 5-day and 20-day averages are simple averages of the last 5 and 20 daily rates. A 30-day change compares the latest rate with the rate 30 days earlier.
